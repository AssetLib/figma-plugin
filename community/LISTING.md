## Description

Browse artwork from your Assetlib workspace, insert it into Figma, and send a selected layer back to a connected placement as a draft. Sync later edits and check Draft, Staging, or Production artwork without losing the placement link.

Publishing happens in the Assetlib console. Assetlib lets teams update compatible app images after an engineer integrates the SDK and ships the initial app release.

The plugin is free. Requires an Assetlib account and workspace; Assetlib is in developer preview. Service pricing and availability: https://www.assetlib.dev/pricing/

Get started: https://github.com/AssetLib/figma-plugin#readme
